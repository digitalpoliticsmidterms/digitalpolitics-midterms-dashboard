The Toss-Up Races

The figures below include Facebook and Instagram combined ad spending by the nominees' own campaign pages in every US Senate, Governor and House of Representatives race that the Cook Political Report rates a “Toss Up.” Outside groups and super PACs are excluded.

Candidate cards also show Google political-ad spend where an official campaign advertiser account has been manually verified. Google and Meta figures remain separate.
